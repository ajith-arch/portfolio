Ajith Alphonse

UI/UX Designer · Salt Lake City, Utah

I turn complex systems into interfaces people can trust. Over five-plus years I’ve designed where the stakes are quiet but real: the payment moment at Flipkart, a fulfillment decision workspace for Amazon operations, and campaign readiness across Adobe’s GenStudio ecosystem.

I’m finishing an MS in Information Systems at the University of Utah, so I care as much about how the system works as how the screen looks. When something is uncertain, I’d rather show that honestly than hide it behind a spinner.

You’d get a designer who starts with the decision, not the screen. I map where people lose confidence, then design the states nobody asks for: pending, failed, partly done, not yet verified.

I prototype until research and engineering can poke real holes in it, and I keep targets and results clearly apart. I’m open to UI/UX and product design roles.

I work closest with research, product and engineering. I share early and messy, label my assumptions, and ask for critique often because it makes the work better.

On Adobe Campaign Readiness I led the design with product designers, UX research, product management and engineering. On APEX I worked alongside UX research, product and engineering across eight connected workflows.

If your product has moving parts like money, inventory, approvals or AI output, I can help it feel calm. I’m good at the first messy map, the flow that holds up under edge cases, and a clickable prototype you can put in front of users.

Bring me the part your team argues about most. That’s usually where the design is.
