------------------------------------------------
2. CORE STORY (LOCKED)
------------------------------------------------

A creator generates something they mostly like.

They do NOT want another completely different generation.

They want to change ONE specific thing while preserving everything
that already works.


Core user thought:
"I like this image. I just don't like that part."


Core design problem:
How can creators communicate WHAT should change,
WHERE it should change,
and WHAT should remain untouched?


Core interaction shift:

FROM:
DESCRIBE → GENERATE → REGENERATE

TOWARD:
GENERATE → SELECT → INSTRUCT → REFINE → COMPARE → KEEP


Keep / change idea:
KEEP 90%
CHANGE 10%

The challenge wasn't generating more.
It was changing less.
